Sleep protocol — implement in this order
Fix wake time first
7:30am every day including weekends. Most impactful intervention. Body pulls sleep onset earlier within 2–3 weeks automatically.
Work cutoff rule
Work stops → wind-down 10–11pm → sleep 11pm. Budget the wind-down explicitly — it's legitimate recovery time, not wasted time.
Magnesium glycinate
300–400mg 30–60 min before bed. Takes the edge off the "can't switch off" feeling.
Room temperature
18–19°C. Sleep quality degrades significantly above 20°C.
No caffeine after 1–2pm
Caffeine half-life 5–7 hrs. A 3pm coffee is 25% active at 11pm.
Apple Watch sleep tracking
Already have this. Wear it overnight to track sleep duration passively. Check the Health app weekly for RHR and HRV trends — log both on Sundays in the tracker.
Why visceral fat score links directly to sleep
Your visceral fat score of 10 is primarily driven by cortisol, not diet. Chronic sleep under 7hrs elevates cortisol, which directly instructs the body to store fat viscerally. Fixing sleep to 7.5hrs consistently is a more direct lever on visceral fat than any dietary change. Target: visceral fat 7 or below by October 2025.
Sauna — 3–4x per week post-session
15–20 min at 80°C+ if gym has one. Strong evidence for cardiovascular health, growth hormone, recovery and sleep improvement. One of the highest-ROI longevity interventions requiring almost no extra time.
Stress & cortisol
Enforce work cutoff, take proper lunch breaks, get outside briefly during the day. Natural light resets cortisol rhythm. Chronic elevated cortisol impairs muscle building, sleep quality, skin health, and testosterone simultaneously.
Stand up and move 2–3 minutes every 45–60 minutes at your desk. Prolonged unbroken sitting is independently associated with visceral fat accumulation even in people who exercise regularly. Set a phone alarm.

Morning mobility routine — 10–12 min
Why you've never been flexible: Football and hurling stretches held 10–20 seconds — below the 45–90s needed for neurological relaxation. You haven't been inflexible, you've never stretched for long enough. Daily practice at proper hold durations and you will touch your toes within 4–6 months.
1
Cat-cow — spine warmup
On all fours. Slowly arch and round, 10 full reps. Breathe into each position. Do not skip — essential warmup.
10 reps · ~1 min
2
Dead hang
Fully relax from a bar. Spine decompresses, shoulders open passively. Essential for desk workers pressing heavy.
2 × 30–45s · 20s rest
3
90/90 hip stretch
Both knees at 90°. Front leg externally rotated, back internally rotated. Sit upright. Switch sides. Critical for squat depth and back health.
60–90s each side
4
Kneeling hip flexor stretch
Kneeling lunge, back knee on floor. Push hips forward, torso upright. Squeeze rear glute to deepen. Most important stretch — primary driver of squat back soreness.
60–90s each side
Key fix for squat back soreness
5
Seated forward fold — hamstrings
Legs straight. Reach toward feet with flat back. Hold at tension, breathe, let it release. Weekly progress marker — note how far you reach each Sunday.
90s · breathe throughout
6-month goal: touch toes
6
Thoracic rotation
Seated. One hand behind head, elbow out. Rotate upper back, follow elbow with eyes. Hips stay still. Counteracts desk posture, protects shoulders in heavy pressing.
10 slow reps each side
